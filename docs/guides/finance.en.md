# Finance Department Role Guide (English) — Top 10 Tasks

1. **Daily Sign-In & Context Check:** Sign in, verify active **Unit (TAL / SAW / VAR)** and **Financial Year** in the top bar.
2. **Switch Language (`EN / हिं`):** Click `EN / हिं` in the top bar to toggle Hindi UI; preference saves to your profile.
3. **Create & Post Journal Voucher:** Open **Finance > Journal Entries** -> **+ New** -> balance Dr = Cr -> Submit -> Approve (above threshold) -> Post.
4. **Bank Reconciliation:** Open **Finance > Bank Reconciliation** -> import statement -> auto-match ledger lines -> confirm difference = ₹ 0.00.
5. **3-Way Purchase Invoice Verification:** Open **Procurement > Purchase Invoices** -> verify PO = GRN = Invoice qty & tax before posting to AP.
6. **Sales Invoice & GST Export:** Open **Sales > Invoices** -> post invoice -> export **GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B / HSN Summary JSON** under **Taxation**.
7. **TDS Payment & Challan:** Open **Finance > TDS** -> generate challan -> record BSR code & challan serial.
8. **Month-End Soft/Hard Close:** Verify Trial Balance difference = 0, Stock Valuation = GL Inventory, and close period cleanly.
9. **Opening Balance Cut-Over (`1-Apr-2027`):** Upload Opening TB/Stock/AR/AP in **System > Hardening** -> dry-run until difference = ₹ 0.00 -> commit & obtain CFO + Dept Head dual sign-off.
10. **Audit Trail & Help (`?`):** Review immutable logs under **Administration > Audit Trail** or click `?` for help & What's New.
